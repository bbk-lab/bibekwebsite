About

I am a licensed civil engineer in Nepal with a Bachelor of Engineering in Civil Engineering from Pokhara University. I currently work as a Site Engineer, planning the daily work, managing labour and machinery, and checking that construction follows the drawings.

Before this I worked as a construction project engineer and a construction supervisor intern, and I volunteer with Engineers Without Borders Nepal on disaster risk reduction workshops. My research interests sit between structures, construction management, and the environment: BIM, risk management, construction safety, and sustainable, resilient infrastructure.

I am applying for a master's degree in Construction Management starting in 2027, and I welcome conversations about research collaboration.
