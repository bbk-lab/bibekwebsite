# Bibek Regmi — Intro Film

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** Bibek's work connects the precision of a drawing to the decisions of a live site.
- **Identity:** The portfolio's warm drafting-paper field, deep construction green, restrained vermilion, and measured dimension marks. Manrope carries bold titles, Newsreader carries reflective phrases, and DM Mono labels the drawings.
- **Hero:** A suspension-bridge line study, treated explicitly as a conceptual engineering drawing rather than a photograph or a claimed project.
- **Motion:** Traced structural lines become planning marks; a diagonal camera move follows the career timeline; drafting contours open into a research network; a certificate is revealed as a physical sheet.
- **Arc:** Quiet precision → purposeful site activity → a concise career progression → questions beyond delivery → service and a resolved name lockup.
- **Payoff:** The drawing's single green cable returns as an underline beneath Bibek's name, changing from an engineering line into a human signature.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape.
- **Runtime:** 32 seconds, seamless visual handoff from the final green line to the opening bridge cable.

## Full shot script

### Shot 01 / 0.00–5.50s / 5.50s — “Plans meet the ground.”

- **Purpose:** Establish the person and the visual relationship between structural intent and real-world construction.
- **Exact content:** `CIVIL ENGINEER · NEPAL` from 0.18–5.50s; `BIBEK` from 0.72–5.50s; `REGMI` from 1.12–5.50s; `Plans meet the ground.` from 2.05–5.50s; drawing labels `FIELD NOTE / 01` and `NEPAL` remain small and legible from 3.15s.
- **Composition:** Warm paper (#f1eee6), broad drafting field with the bridge across the right two-thirds, name cropped large on the left. Thin green cable, dark ink towers, orange dimension ticks. Safe inset 6%.
- **Artistic idea:** The suspension cable is the hero stroke: it traces itself, pulls the eye through the name, then scales to become the next scene's planning route. The bridge is an illustrative line study, not represented as Bibek's project.
- **Timed choreography:** 0.00s hairline datum strikes; 0.42s first tower snaps in; 0.86s second tower snaps in; 1.30s cable draws in a single confident arc. 0.20–1.65s label/name arrive as two discrete, 0.4s-apart type beats. At 2.15s deck line closes the study; at 3.05s orange span tick and caption appear; from 4.65s the cable pushes toward centre and enlarges, continuing through the cut.
- **Transition contract:** The green cable remains on the same screen-space curve at the cut; its centre segment matches the planning route in Shot 02. The line continues moving while paper geometry changes beneath it.
- **Assets and technique:** Original SVG bridge line study, portfolio palette and fonts; no photography. SVG strokes and masked/transform type.
- **Sound intent:** Instrumental music, a restrained struck-percussion pulse. Cut at 5.50s; beat alignment pending music generation.

### Shot 02 / 5.50–11.50s / 6.00s — “A plan becomes a workday.”

- **Purpose:** Show the substance of current site-engineering practice, without pretending to show an actual site.
- **Exact content:** `SITE ENGINEER` and `BANDAN BHAGWATI NIRMAN SEWA PVT. LTD.` from 5.80–11.50s; sequential labels `DAILY WORK PLAN` at 6.50s, `LABOUR + MACHINERY` at 7.70s, `DRAWINGS → EXECUTION` at 8.90s; `JULY 2025 — PRESENT` at 9.80s.
- **Composition:** Bird's-eye abstract plan sheet, offset to lower right; large typographic title takes upper-left quarter, three labels occupy non-overlapping lower-left positions. Green route line rides through the plan; orange ticks establish measured scale.
- **Artistic idea:** A single route line gains scheduled nodes; a blank field becomes a sequence of decisions rather than a fake project photograph.
- **Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.45s route line continues from previous scene; 0.50s paper grid snaps into a skewed technical plane. 1.00s title is unmasked. 1.45s first label connects to a tick; 2.65s second label replaces its place with a second tick; 3.85s third label ties the route to a drawing edge; 4.30s date resolves. At 5.20s ticks compress into a horizontal datum, carrying through the boundary.
- **Transition contract:** The compressed datum becomes the first line of the career timeline. The plan's paper plane pivots away as the timeline swings into depth.
- **Assets and technique:** Original vector grid and schedule-like marks; no site photographs or fabricated project metrics. Camera-plane rotation, tracing strokes, staggered labels.
- **Sound intent:** Instrumental continues; the label changes should sit on distinct percussive accents if available.

- **Composition:** Deep green fills frame; oversized cream name at centre, with the cable/underline running across the lower third. A slim orange datum punctuates the left end. Unlike the opening technical sheet, this final card is nearly typographic and centred.
- **Artistic idea:** The same bridge cable that began as measured structure becomes an underline: a precise line now associated with a person's name and a point of view.
- **Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.35s carried green line sweeps to the centre and settles as underline. 0.20–0.85s name resolves in two tightly spaced type impacts. 1.05s profession lands below; 1.60s point-of-view sentence appears. 2.60s tiny orange registration mark moves along the underline toward the centre; at 3.60s the name field contracts into the opening cable curve and scale, handing off into the first frame without a blank.
- **Transition contract:** End-frame green curve, point, and scale match Shot 01 opening line; the loop reads as the same engineering line continuing through a new drawing.
- **Assets and technique:** Typography, geometric line, palette inversion; Manrope, Newsreader, DM Mono.
- **Sound intent:** Music resolves with the loop boundary aligned to its final return; confirm against generated bed before implementation.
